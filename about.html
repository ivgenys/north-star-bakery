The Story of North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery began in 2019 when bakers Maya and Tomas Lin started hauling forty loaves of sourdough in wooden crates to the Northeast Minneapolis weekend farmers market. By 2021, weekend market regulars were reserving all forty loaves before 9:00 AM, which gave Maya and Tomas Lin the footing to lease our permanent brick shop on Oak Street.

Behind our front register on Oak Street, an open kitchen window lets morning coffee customers and neighborhood kids watch our bakers shape boules on maple benches and load the stone deck ovens.

Our Regional Sourcing Philosophy

  • Stone-Milled Organic Grains: Our hard red spring wheat, rye, and ancient spelt are cold-milled each week at Sunrise Heritage Mill in Wisconsin to preserve the natural wheat germ oils and nutty aroma.
  • Pasture-Raised Dairy & Eggs: All laminated pastries and cake batters use high-fat cultured butter from Saint Croix Valley creameries and cage-free eggs from Northfield farms.
  • Seasonal Fruit & Wildflower Honey: We sweeten our loaves and compotes with raw basswood and clover honey from Minnesota apiaries and orchard fruit harvested at peak ripeness.

Staff Highlights: Meet Our Bakers

Maya Lin, Co-Founder & Head Bread Baker

Co-founder Maya Lin manages our ten-year-old sourdough starters, grain hydration schedules, and 4:00 AM deck oven bakes after training at neighborhood bakeries in Portland and Minneapolis.

Tomas Lin, Co-Founder & Executive Pastry Chef

Tomas Lin runs the morning pastry bench, where he laminates cultured butter into croissant dough and tests weekly specials like our Brown Butter Cardamom Morning Buns.

Elena Rostova, Lead Cake Decorator & Events Coordinator

Elena Rostova frosts our custom celebration cakes and coordinates directly with families and office event planners on cake flavors, allergy notes, and pickup schedules.
