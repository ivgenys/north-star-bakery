House Specialty: Signature Honey Oat Sourdough

Product Categories & Counter Pricing Ranges

Every loaf and pastry on our menu is mixed in daily batches on Oak Street, while custom celebration cakes and catering boxes are baked to order with 48 to 72 hours notice. We take payment at the counter instead of using an online checkout cart, so please submit our pre-order and inquiry form to hold loaves, pastry boxes, or cakes for pickup.

Artisan Sourdough & Hearth Breads ($6.50 to $11.00)

Our bread bakers mix organic Midwest wheat and rye with our ten-year-old sourdough starter and bake each boule and batard on steam-injected stone decks.

  • Traditional French Baguette ($6.50): Crisp golden crust with a light, chewy interior for soup or sandwiches.
  • Oak Street Country Boule ($7.50): Classic white and whole-wheat sourdough leavened with our ten-year-old house starter.
  • Seeded Multigrain Batard ($9.00): Rolled in toasted sunflower, flax, pumpkin, and black sesame seeds.
  • Signature Honey Oat Sourdough ($9.50): Soft porridge crumb sweetened lightly with Minnesota wildflower honey.
  • Rosemary Sea Salt Focaccia Slab ($8.50 half / $11.00 full): Cold-proofed olive oil flatbread topped with fresh rosemary needles and flaky sea salt.

Morning Pastries & Viennoiserie ($4.25 to $6.75 each, or $24.00 to $36.00 per half-dozen box)

Each morning our pastry bench laminates high-fat Saint Croix Valley butter into croissant and danish doughs alongside warm buttermilk scones.

  • Cultured Butter Croissant ($4.50): Three-day laminated pastry with honeycomb layers and a shattering crust.
  • Dark Chocolate Pain au Chocolat ($5.25): Filled with two batons of 70% bittersweet chocolate.
  • Brown Butter Cardamom Morning Bun ($5.00): Twisted brioche croissant dough tossed in freshly ground cardamom and orange sugar.
  • Seasonal Berry & Cream Cheese Danish ($5.75): Crisp puff pastry topped with whipped vanilla bean Neufchatel and local berries.
  • Savory Gruyere & Chive Scone ($4.75): Tender buttermilk scone baked with aged Gruyere cheese and cracked black pepper.

Celebration Cakes & Event Catering Platters ($38.00 to $95.00)

Our decorators frost every layer cake by hand and bake event platters to order, so please send cake and catering requests 48 to 72 hours before pickup.

  • 6-Inch Three-Layer Seasonal Berry Cake ($38.00 to $45.00, serves 8 to 10): Vanilla buttermilk sponge layered with berry compote and Swiss meringue buttercream.
  • 9-Inch Dark Chocolate Espresso Ganache Cake ($58.00 to $68.00, serves 16 to 20): Rich cocoa crumb with espresso fudge frosting and chocolate curls.
  • Commuter & Event Pastry Platter ($42.00 to $95.00, 12 to 30 pieces): Custom mix of mini croissants, morning buns, and scones boxed for group gatherings.
